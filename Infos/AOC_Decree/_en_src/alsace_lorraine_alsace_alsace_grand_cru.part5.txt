@@H1 Altenberg de Wolxheim
@@NAT Altenberg de Wolxheim
The Altenberg de Wolxheim grand cru is set around a peak of oolitic limestone. The slopes delimit a full-south-facing amphitheatre, sheltered from the prevailing winds and from the north-east. The result is a warm, dry summer mesoclimate, offset by marls at depth which ensure a regular water supply for the vine.
@@HB
@@HUM1
As a translation of this usage, the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Wolxheim” was recognised in 1992. The wines come from parcels that have been the subject of a rigorous and precise delimitation inspired by the historical cores of production. This recognition was built around the white varieties. Basically, the only varieties selected are the following: riesling B, gewurztraminer Rs, pinot gris G, muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B. They are vinified and marketed under their respective denomination.
Since the law of 5 July 1972, the wines must be bottled within the Bas-Rhin and Haut-Rhin departments, traditionally in the “Vin du Rhin”-type flute described in the decree of 1955.
The vineyard, trained on high trellises in order to benefit from an optimal leaf area, is subject to more restrictive production conditions than the controlled designation of origin “Alsace”. The minimum planting density is 4,500 plants per hectare. The grapes are harvested by hand. Wines benefiting from the terms “vendanges tardives” and “sélection de grains nobles” come from overripe and fragile grapes, also harvested by hand. Wines claiming these terms must undergo a minimum ageing period of 18 months.

## XI. – Transitional measures
### 1° – Delimited parcel area
On a transitional basis, vine-planted parcels excluded from the delimited parcel area “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf”, as identified by their cadastral references and their area by the competent national committee of the National Institute of Origin and Quality at its session of 6 and 7 September 2006, may benefit for their harvest from the right to the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf” until they are grubbed up and at the latest until the 2031 harvest inclusive, provided that they meet the conditions set out in the present specification.
### 2° – Training method
For white wines: the planting density and the trellising and pruning rules, with the exception of the rule relating to the distance between the carrying wire and the attachment wire, do not apply to vines planted before 1 September 2000. These vines are trained with a maximum of 18 free eyes per plant. They continue to benefit, for their harvest, from the right to the controlled designation of origin until they are grubbed up and at the latest until the 2025 harvest.
## XII. – Rules of presentation and labelling
### 1° – General provisions
Wines for which, under the terms of the present specification, one of the controlled designations of origin covered by the present specification is claimed and which are presented under the said appellation may not be declared after the harvest, offered to the public, dispatched, put up for sale or sold unless, in the harvest declaration, in advertisements, on leaflets, labels, invoices and any containers whatsoever, the aforesaid controlled designation of origin is entered.
### 2° – Particular provisions
a) – Any optional indication whose use, under Community provisions, may be regulated by the Member States is entered on the labels in characters whose dimensions, in height, width and thickness, are not greater than those of the characters of the appellation.
b) – The use of two or more customary denominations on the same label is prohibited.
c) – The vintage must appear together with the name of the appellation in the harvest and stock declarations, on the accompanying documents, in advertisements, on leaflets, labels, invoices and any containers whatsoever.
d) – White wines for which, under the terms of the present specification, one of the 51 controlled designations of origin “Alsace Grand Cru – lieu-dit” is claimed, with the exception of the terms Vendanges Tardives and Sélection de Grains Nobles, and which are presented under the said appellation, may not be offered to the public, dispatched, put up for sale or sold unless the indication of the sugar content, as defined by European regulations, is entered in very conspicuous characters in advertisements, on leaflets, labels, invoices and any containers whatsoever.
e) – Wines liable to benefit from the terms “vendanges tardives” or “sélection de grains nobles” must be presented:
- with the indication of the vintage;
- and with one of the following customary denominations: Gewurztraminer, Muscat, Muscat Ottonel, Pinot Gris, Riesling.
# CHAPTER II
## I. – Declaration obligations
#### 1. Prior declaration of parcel assignment
A prior declaration of parcel assignment must be lodged with the defence and management body before 1 March of the year of harvest. This declaration specifies:
- the identity of the operator;
- the EVV or SIRET number;
- the identity of the operator receiving the grapes, where applicable;
- for each parcel: the cadastral reference, the area, the grape variety.
The operator may declare to the defence and management body that they renounce producing the controlled designation of origin at the latest on 15 August preceding the harvest. They specify the PDO finally produced on the parcels concerned.
#### 2. Prior declaration of parcel assignment for vines liable to benefit from the term “vendanges tardives” or “sélection de grains nobles”
The prior declaration of parcel assignment is lodged with the defence and management body before 1 March of the year of harvest. This declaration specifies:
- the identity of the operator;
- the EVV or SIRET number;
- the identity of the operator receiving the grapes, where applicable;
- for each parcel: the cadastral reference, the area, the grape variety.
#### 3. Declaration relating to vinification vat capacity
Every operator vinifying wines of the controlled designation of origin sends the defence and management body, at the latest on 31 August of the year of harvest, a declaration relating to vinification vat capacity. The cellar plan annexed to the identification declaration may serve as the declaration relating to vinification vat capacity. This declaration is renewable by tacit renewal, unless changes are notified by the operator before 31 August of the year of harvest. In the event of a sale of grapes, this declaration specifies the destination of the harvest, in particular on the basis of the inter-trade contract entered into.
#### 4. Claim declaration
The claim declaration is lodged with the defence and management body at least 15 days before the first exit of products from the vinification cellar and at the latest 60 days after the date of placing on the market for the consumer.
It indicates:
- the designation claimed;
- the volume of the wine;
- the EVV or SIRET number;
- the name and address of the applicant;
- the place of storage of the wine.
It is accompanied by a copy of the harvest declaration and, as the case may be, a copy of the production declaration or an extract of the stock accounts for buyers of grapes and musts.
#### 5. Prior declaration of collections
Every operator wishing to sell in bulk a wine benefiting from the controlled designation of origin sends the approved control body a transaction declaration for the lot concerned between the day the transaction is contracted and at least eight days before collection. If the volume actually collected differs by more or less than 10 % from the volume indicated when the contract was concluded, the operator makes a correction available to the approved control body.
#### 6. Fallback declaration
Every operator marketing a wine benefiting from the controlled designation of origin under a more general appellation must declare it to the defence and management body and to the approved control body within a maximum period of 15 days after this fallback.
#### 7. Downgrading declaration
Every operator carrying out a downgrading of wines benefiting from the controlled designation of origin must declare it to the defence and management body and to the approved control body within a maximum period of 15 days after this downgrading.
