# CHAPTER III
## I. – Main points to be checked and evaluation methods
| MAIN POINTS TO BE CHECKED | EVALUATION METHODS |
| **A – STRUCTURAL RULES** | < |
| A1 – Location of operators within the immediate proximity area | Documentary check |
| A2 – Belonging of the planted parcels to the delimited area | – Documentary check: up-to-date CVI parcel record; – Check in the field |
| A3 – Claimable production potential (grape varieties and proportion rules, planting density, plant material) | – Documentary check; – Check in the field |
| A4 – Processing, ageing, packaging and storage facilities | < |
| Vat capacity | – Documentary check: general plan of the storage places; – On-site check |
| Ageing (temperature control and length of ageing) | – Documentary check: declaration of release for consumption or bottling register; – On-site check |
| State of upkeep of the cellar and equipment (hygiene) | On-site check |
| Protected storage place and storage conditions (T °C) | On-site check |
| **B – RULES LINKED TO THE PRODUCTION CYCLE** | < |
| B1 – Management of the vineyard | < |
| Pruning | Check in the field |
| Average maximum load per parcel | Check in the field |
| Cultivation and sanitary condition of the vine (sanitary condition of foliage and berries, upkeep of the soil, upkeep of the trellising) | Check in the field |
| B2 – Harvest, transport and ripeness of the grapes | < |
| Ripeness of the grapes | – Documentary check: records of ripeness monitoring; – Check in the field |
| B3 – Processing, elaboration, ageing, packaging, storage | < |
| Oenological practices or treatments (enrichment, prohibited practices…) | – Documentary check: enrichment, acidification and de-acidification register; – On-site check |
| Stock accounts, analytical traceability | Documentary check: keeping of registers, analysis reports |
| B4 – Harvest declaration and claim declaration | < |
| Missing vines | – Documentary check (keeping the list up to date); – Check in the field |
| Authorised yield | Documentary check: check of declarations, monitoring of authorisations granted by the INAO services after their investigation of the operator’s individual request |
| VSI, volumes harvested in excess of the authorised yield | Documentary check: monitoring of destruction certificates |
| Claim declaration | – Documentary and on-site check: compliance with procedures and time limits, consistency with the harvest and production declarations; – Check of the release of products into circulation |
| **C – CHECKS ON THE PRODUCTS** | < |
| Non-packaged wines (at transaction or collection) | Analytical and organoleptic examination |
| Packaged wines (before or after preparation for release for consumption) | Analytical and organoleptic examination |
| Non-packaged wines intended for dispatch outside the national territory | Analytical and organoleptic examination of all lots |
| **D – PRESENTATION OF THE PRODUCTS** | < |
| Labelling | On-site check |

This control body is accredited in accordance with standard 45011.
Compliance with the present specification is checked by a third-party body offering guarantees of competence, impartiality and independence, on behalf of the INAO, on the basis of an approved control plan.
The control plan recalls the self-checks carried out by operators on their own activity and the internal checks carried out under the responsibility of the defence and management body. It indicates the external checks carried out by the third-party body as well as the analytical and organoleptic examinations.
All checks are carried out by sampling. Non-packaged wines intended for dispatch outside the national territory are subject to systematic analytical and organoleptic checks.
