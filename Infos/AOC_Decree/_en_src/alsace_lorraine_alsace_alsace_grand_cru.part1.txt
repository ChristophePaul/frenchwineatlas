# CHAPTER I
## I. – Name of the designations
Only wines meeting the specific provisions set out below may claim one of the controlled designations of origin “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim”, “Alsace grand cru Brand”, “Alsace grand cru Eichberg”, “Alsace grand cru Florimont”, “Alsace grand cru Froehn”, “Alsace grand cru Furstentum”, “Alsace grand cru Geisberg”, “Alsace grand cru Gloeckelberg”, “Alsace grand cru Goldert”, “Alsace grand cru Hatschbourg”, “Alsace grand cru Hengst”, “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf”, “Alsace grand cru Kanzlerberg”, “Alsace grand cru Kessler”, “Alsace grand cru Kirchberg de Ribeauvillé”, “Alsace grand cru Kitterlé”, “Alsace grand cru Mambourg”, “Alsace grand cru Mandelberg”, “Alsace grand cru Marckrain”, “Alsace grand cru Ollwiller”, “Alsace grand cru Osterberg”, “Alsace grand cru Pfersigberg”, “Alsace grand cru Pfingstberg”, “Alsace grand cru Rangen”, “Alsace grand cru Rosacker”, “Alsace grand cru Saering”, “Alsace grand cru Schlossberg”, “Alsace grand cru Schoenenbourg”, “Alsace grand cru Sommerberg”, “Alsace grand cru Sonnenglanz”, “Alsace grand cru Spiegel”, “Alsace grand cru Sporen”, “Alsace grand cru Steinert”, “Alsace grand cru Steingrubler”, “Alsace grand cru Vorbourg”, “Alsace grand cru Wineck-Schlossberg”, “Alsace grand cru Zinnkoepflé”, “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergbieten”, “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Wolxheim”, “Alsace grand cru Bruderthal”, “Alsace grand cru Engelberg”, “Alsace grand cru Frankstein”, “Alsace grand cru Kastelberg”, “Alsace grand cru Kirchberg de Barr”, “Alsace grand cru Moenchberg”, “Alsace grand cru Muenchberg”, “Alsace grand cru Praelatenberg”, “Alsace grand cru Steinklotz”, “Alsace grand cru Wiebelsberg”, “Alsace grand cru Winzenberg”, “Alsace grand cru Zotzenberg”, initially recognised by the decrees of 20 November 1975, 23 November 1983, 17 December 1992 and 12 January 2007.
This specification is drawn up in accordance with the provisions of ordinance no. 45-2675 of 2 November 1945 and without prejudice to law no. 72-628 of 5 July 1972.
## II. – Geographical denominations and complementary terms
1° – The name of the appellation may be supplemented by one of the customary denominations, provided that the wines come exclusively from the grape varieties that may be designated under the denomination concerned and meet the production conditions set out in the present specification:
| DENOMINATION | GRAPE VARIETIES |
| Gewurztraminer | gewurztraminer Rs |
| Muscat | muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B |
| Muscat Ottonel | muscat ottonel B |
| Pinot gris | pinot gris G |
| Riesling | riesling B |
| Sylvaner | sylvaner B |
| Pinot noir | pinot noir N |
2° – The name of the appellation may be supplemented by the terms “vendanges tardives” or “sélection de grains nobles” for wines meeting the production conditions set for these terms in the present specification, with the exception of wines from harvests gathered in the production area of the appellation “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf” and coming from the pinot gris G variety. These terms must be supplemented by one of the following customary denominations as defined in 1° above: Gewurztraminer, Muscat, Muscat Ottonel, Pinot gris, Riesling.
## III. – Types of product
The controlled designations of origin covered by the present specification, with the exception of the controlled designations of origin “Alsace grand cru Hengst”, “Alsace grand cru Kirchberg de Barr” and “Alsace grand cru Vorbourg”, are reserved for still white wines.
The controlled designations of origin “Alsace grand cru Hengst”, “Alsace grand cru Kirchberg de Barr” and “Alsace grand cru Vorbourg” are reserved for still white and red wines.

## V. – Grape varieties
### 1° – Grape varieties
a) – The wines come:
- for white wines:
> − either from a single one of the following varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B, pinot gris G, riesling B;
> − or from a blend of the following varieties: muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B;
- for red wines: from the pinot noir N variety.
b) – Wines of the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim” come:
- either from a single one of the following varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, pinot gris G, riesling B;
- or from a blend of the following varieties:
> − main variety: riesling B;
> − complementary varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, pinot gris G;
> − accessory varieties: for vines planted before 26 March 2005, chasselas B, muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B, pinot blanc B, pinot noir N.
c) – Wines of the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Zotzenberg” come from a single one of the following varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, pinot gris G, riesling B, sylvaner B.
d) – Wines of the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf” come:
- either from a single one of the following varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, pinot gris G, riesling B;
- or from a blend of the following varieties:
> − main variety: gewurztraminer Rs;
> − complementary variety: riesling B;
> − accessory varieties: muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B, pinot gris G.
e) – Wines liable to benefit from the terms “vendanges tardives” or “sélection de grains nobles” come from the following varieties: gewurztraminer Rs, muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs, muscat ottonel B, pinot gris G, riesling B.
### 2° – Proportion rules on the holding
a) – For wines made from several varieties and liable to benefit from the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim”, the proportion of the riesling B variety may not be less than 50 % nor more than 70 % of the planting.
The proportion of each of the complementary varieties may not be less than 10 % nor more than 25 % of the planting.
The proportion of all the accessory varieties together may not exceed 10 % of the planting.
Compliance of the planting is assessed over all the parcels of the holding producing the wine of the appellation.
b) – For wines made from several varieties and liable to benefit from the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf”, the proportion of the gewurztraminer Rs variety may not be less than 60 % nor more than 80 % of the planting.
The proportion of the riesling B variety may not be less than 10 % nor more than 40 % of the planting.
The proportion of the pinot gris G variety may not exceed 30 % of the planting.
The proportion of the varieties muscat à petits grains blancs B, muscat à petits grains roses Rs and muscat ottonel B may not exceed 10 % of the planting.
Compliance of the planting is assessed over all the parcels of the holding producing the wine of the appellation, or in relation to all the parcels used to elaborate a multi-variety blended cuvée of this appellation.
## VI. – Management of the vineyard
### 1° – Training methods
a) – Planting density
- For the production of white wine:
The vines have a minimum planting density of 4,500 plants per hectare.
Vines intended for the production of wines liable to benefit from the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim” and planted from 26 March 2005 have a minimum planting density of 5,500 plants per hectare.
- For the production of red wine:
Vines intended for the production of red wines liable to benefit from the controlled designations of origin “Alsace grand cru Hengst” or “Alsace grand cru Vorbourg” have a minimum planting density of 5,500 plants per hectare.
Vines intended for the production of red wines liable to benefit from the controlled designation of origin “Alsace grand cru Kirchberg de Barr” have a minimum planting density of 5,000 plants per hectare.
- Whatever the type of wine:
The vines may not have a spacing between rows greater than 2 metres.
The vines have a spacing within the row of between 0.75 metre minimum and 1.50 metres maximum.
From 25 October 2011, the grubbing-up of rows within a parcel may not result in a spacing between the widest rows greater than 3 metres.
b) – Pruning rules
For white wines, the vines are pruned by single or double Guyot pruning with a maximum of 18 free eyes per plant.
For red wines, the vines are pruned by single or double Guyot pruning with a maximum of 14 free eyes per plant.
c) – Trellising and foliage height rules
The foliage is trained on a vertical trellis. The height of trellised foliage may not be less than 0.675 times the spacing between rows. This height is measured between the attachment wire and the upper limit of the foliage after trimming.
The distance between the carrying wire and the attachment wire may not exceed 0.30 metre.
d) – Average maximum load per parcel
For white wines, the average maximum load per parcel is set at 8,500 kilograms per hectare.
For red wines, the average maximum load per parcel is set at 7,000 kilograms per hectare.
e) – Threshold of missing vines
The percentage of dead or missing vine plants referred to in Article D. 645-4 of the Rural and Maritime Fishing Code is set at 20 %.
f) – Cultivation condition of the vine
The parcels are managed so as to ensure a good overall cultivation condition of the vine, in particular its sanitary condition and the upkeep of its soil.
### 2° – Other cultivation practices
a) – In order to preserve the characteristics of the soils, which constitute a fundamental element of the terroir:
- Headlands wider than one metre are grassed, with the exception of vines that cannot be mechanised, known locally as “Kammerle”.
- Partial or total covering of the soil and of the vines with sheeting is prohibited.
b) – Vine plantings and replantings are carried out with plant material all of whose components are produced in protected zones or have undergone hot-water treatment as defined by the order in force relating to the control of flavescence dorée.
### 3° – Irrigation
Irrigation is prohibited.
## VII. – Harvest, transport and ripeness of the grapes
### 1° – Harvest
a) – The wines come from grapes harvested at good ripeness.
The date of the start of the harvest is set in accordance with the provisions of Article D. 645-6 of the Rural and Maritime Fishing Code and of Article 2 of ordinance no. 45-2675 of 2 November 1945 on the definition of the designations of origin of the wines of Alsace.
b) – The wines come from grapes harvested by hand.
c) – Containers used for transporting the harvest must be sheltered from bad weather.
Washing of the harvest containers after each emptying is compulsory.
